ASTROVITA — Visual Assets Pack
================================

Important usage notes
---------------------
1. The ASTROVITA logo and all anatomical/space artwork in this pack are AI-generated
   concept visuals. They are not official NASA assets, validated medical illustrations,
   or anatomically accurate clinical references. Use them for decorative UI only.
2. The organ images are NOT 3D models and do NOT enable organ picking. The real GLB/GLTF
   male and female models must be supplied separately and tested before claiming 3D
   organ selection works.
3. No NASA insignia is included. A NASA-looking mark generated by AI is not authentic
   and should not be used. NASA's brand guidance restricts use of its insignia and
   logotype; do not imply NASA sponsorship or endorsement. Review: 
   https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/brand-guidelines/
   Media guidance: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
4. For real NASA mission photography, select individual files from NASA's official
   image/media collections and follow their media-use guidance, including credit and
   third-party copyright checks.
5. Crop dimensions vary by asset. All files are JPEG except branding PNGs.

Suggested web paths
-------------------
Copy the images folder into your React project's public/assets/astrovita/ folder.
Example: public/assets/astrovita/organs/heart.jpg
